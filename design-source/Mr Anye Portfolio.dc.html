About
Anye · 2026

Nine years taking brands from the first meeting to the finished thing.

I’m Ubong Anye. Everybody calls me Mr. Anye — friends started it long before clients did, and it stuck. Lately a few clients have started saying Mr. Stramm, which is newer and I’m still deciding how I feel about it.

I started in visual communication — first on the Nigerian Presidency’s digital channels, then in advocacy — moved through brand strategy and SaaS product design, and ended up running my own practice because I kept wanting to see an idea through to the finished thing instead of handing over files and hoping. That’s Stramm Creative for brand and campaign work, and Cenic AI Studio for film. Nine years in, seven of them delivering remotely.

Away from client work I read far too much African mythology, make a podcast nobody asked for, and occasionally build software because complaining wasn’t working.

Most creative directors stop making things for themselves. I never managed it. No client, no brief, no deadline — which is exactly why these are the ones worth reading.

001 · Product

Kobo — money, but in a chat

An invoicing and business-records product that lives inside a WhatsApp thread, built for Nigerian small businesses. I designed the product, wrote every string in it, and set the rule governing when it’s allowed to be funny and when it absolutely is not.

002 · Animation

The Severed Sky — twenty gods, still counting

An original animated franchise built on African myth as structure rather than costume. Twenty characters across two cohorts, one locked house style, and a season shape I’ve been quietly building for years.

003 · Apparel

Victor of Calvary — quiet faith, loud fit

A covert Christian streetwear label. One symbol per face, nothing preachy on the garment, everything carried on the hang-tag. Drop 01 shipped as TETELESTAI.

004 · Film

Tiny Hands — a phone ad nobody commissioned

A 60-second AI-generated spec film for the TECNO CAMON 50 Pro. Concept, 16-frame storyboard, prompt system, generation and final edit — all of it mine, none of it briefed.
